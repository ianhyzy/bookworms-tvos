# Bookworms for Apple TV

<a href="https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bookworms-more-social-reading/id6832390609"><img src="badges/app-store.svg" alt="Download on the App Store" height="40"></a> <a href="https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bookworms-more-social-reading/id6832390609"><img src="badges/mac-app-store.svg" alt="Download on the Mac App Store" height="40"></a> <a href="https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bookworms-ebook-display/id6813137450"><img src="badges/apple-tv.svg" alt="Download on Apple TV" height="40"></a>

Bookworms for Apple TV is a beautiful app that helps make e-reading more social IRL.

Bookworms displays your digital library and reading activity on the big screen. In lieu of a bookshelf guests can snoop the spines of, they can look at your TV when it's not in use.

Bookworms also supports comparing your reading activity and history with a user you follow on Hardcover, so you can compare each other's top rated reads, book reviews, and ~~argue~~ talk about your reading tastes.

Currently, Bookworms can pull data from Hardcover and Calibre Web Automated.

Goodreads and StoryGraph prefer to hoard your reading data to themselves. However, you can [import data from those apps to Hardcover](https://hardcover.app/pages/faq).

Bookworms also has an OLED-aware Ambient mode that dims the screen and cycles through selected views on a schedule you choose.
