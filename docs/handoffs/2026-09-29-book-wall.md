# Book Wall development handoff

Book Wall keeps its RealityKit scene behind native tvOS controls and renders it through `RealityRenderer`, its only host. Launch arguments tune the renderer for diagnosis; they have no Settings interface or persisted preference.

Read [Book Wall models, animation, and local spines](../BOOK_WALL.md) for the implementation map, current motion values, coordinate conventions, lighting, and renderer settings. Read the [RealityRenderer results](2026-09-30-wall-realityrenderer-results.md) for measured configurations, color comparison evidence, verification status, and pending experiments. [VIEWS.md](../../VIEWS.md) describes user-facing behavior.

## Current implementation

- `BookWallRendererHost` renders the shared `BookWallScene`: prepared books, camera, physics, lighting, ribbon, shelf labels, and flight planning. The same model falls, settles, responds to focus, opens, idles, and returns.
- The backdrop uses baked, unlit resting and detail images. No light casts a shadow; soft planes provide contact and detail shadows. Preserve the baked color path when changing presentation settings.
- Native controls retain directional focus and sidebar access. Detail text appears after the book's flight succeeds. Cancellation and revision checks prevent stale presentation, and closing details restores the selected spine.
- Preparation derives local spines from library metadata and cover artwork before browsing. Remote movement does not refresh the library or regenerate prepared assets.

## Constraints to preserve

- Keep source artwork intact, preserve its aspect ratio, and follow the documented UV directions. Keep the casing, artwork corner profiles, spine wall, and page inset aligned.
- Keep the shallow edge roll independent of corner radius. Preserve a hardcover silhouette with crisp corners, visible covering and paper, and restrained motion.
- Update model bounds, collision allowance, flight clearance, and camera framing together when changing dimensions. Preserve the current idle pose and velocity when returning a book.
- Keep book, camera, and lighting transitions on the shared scene clock. Extend frustum fitting and return velocity handling before adding another animated rotation axis.
- Let the native focus engine own directional movement. Correct projection and layout errors without focus writes or screen-edge clamps. Follow [focus and remote navigation](../FOCUS_NAVIGATION.md).
- Keep renderer experiments behind the [renderer host launch arguments](../BOOK_WALL.md#renderer-host). The defaults are 1080p internal and output sizes, a 60 Hz request, and 4× multisampling. Making 30 Hz or a lower internal resolution the default requires the owner's approval.

## Verification boundaries

Keep source checks, interaction checks, physical appearance, and frame presentation separate. A build or configured display-link rate does not establish smooth frames. Screenshots show captured poses and materials; they do not verify the full flight, fade, or idle envelope. Accessibility audits do not establish actual VoiceOver behavior or motion comfort.

Use [Book Wall checks](../TESTING.md#book-wall-checks) and [performance diagnosis](../PERFORMANCE_DIAGNOSIS.md#book-wall-motion) for repeatable verification. Confirm exclusive Apple TV access before device work, script remote input, and restore the ordinary local build afterward. Keep coverage, automatic test video, and checker injection out of timing comparisons. Device installation and Instruments availability require separate checks.

Store dated measurements, captures, source identities, and verification limits in ignored `.local/` result folders. Match each result to the candidate it evaluated. Report missing, failed, or blocked checks as such; test source and compilation are not execution evidence. Keep owner visual approval separate from automated results.

Inspect the current working tree before continuing, preserve unrelated changes, and update affected implementation references with behavior changes. Follow [contributor instructions](../../AGENTS.md) for linting, builds, and authorized test runs. For documentation-only work, verify accuracy and links without claiming runtime verification. Publishing and distribution follow the separate [release workflow](../RELEASING.md).
