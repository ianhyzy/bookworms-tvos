# Wood textures

The app bundles these diffuse textures by Jenelle van Heerden from Poly Haven:

- `WalnutWood`: [Black Walnut Veneer 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/black_walnut_veneer_01).
- `OakWood`: [White Oak Veneer](https://polyhaven.com/a/white_oak_veneer).

Both assets use [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license). The bundled files are the original 2K JPEG diffuse maps. SwiftUI blends them over the appearance palette to keep the background quiet and the captions readable. The app does not download textures at runtime.
