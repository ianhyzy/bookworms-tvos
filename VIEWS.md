# tvOS views

This file defines the available views and user options for tvOS. See [data sources](DATASOURCES.md) for data rules and limits.

## Main views

Six views are available on every supported Apple TV. **Book Wall** is also available on Apple TV 4K (2nd generation) and later. All available views except **Book Wall** are enabled by default; turn Book Wall on in **Settings → Views**. **Settings → Views** shows or hides each view in the sidebar, including **My Shelf**; the last visible view can't be hidden, and hiding the current view moves to the first visible one. Preferences saved before a default view existed gain it once; turning it off afterward persists. The Ambient page chooses ambient views separately. Navigation uses native tvOS sidebar navigation (`TabView` with `.sidebarAdaptable`): a collapsed pill at the top left displays the current screen title and icon, expanding into a frosted glass sidebar overlay when navigating left from the leading item or pressing Back (<). The sidebar lists all enabled views, then **Ambient** and **Settings**. On tvOS 27 and later, the user's Hardcover avatar and display name appear above the list. Per-view choices appear on the view itself, in the top-right header for Year in Review, Compare Shelves, and Book Club, so they can change without leaving the view. **Settings** holds library, source, and app-wide choices in four sections: **Views**, **Shelf**, **Sources**, and **General**.

| View | Data source and content | User options |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **My Shelf** | Combined library from enabled Hardcover and Calibre Web Automated (CWA) sources. | In **Settings → Shelf**, choose **Default**, **Owned Books**, or **All Books** (default). **Default** and **All Books** show up to three books from your Hardcover **Currently Reading** shelf first, including audiobooks, most recently started first. **Default** then shows read books; **All Books** shows every other book. Sort by **Date Read** (default), **Your Rating**, **Publication Year**, **Title**, **Author**, **Length**, or **Hot Takes** (largest gap between your rating and the community average first; the caption then shows both ratings, such as "You ★5 · Community ★3.2"); reverse the order; change **Books shown** with the minus and plus buttons in steps of five, from 5 to 40 (default 40); choose **Progress bars**: **Don't show**, **Horizontal**, or **Vertical** (default). |
| **Year in Review** | Books from enabled sources with a recorded finish date, grouped by calendar year. Shows the year's books read, page total, average rating, busiest month, books per month, top genres, most-read author (when one has at least two books), and each format's icon and count; a book read in two formats counts toward both. A **Records** panel names the longest book, the shortest (when two or more have page counts), and the highest-rated; the latest finish wins a tie. A cover shelf below lists the year's books earliest first, with the same caption as My Shelf underneath. | In the top-right header, choose a year from every year with a finished book. The view opens on the most recent year each session. Open details from a cover. Enable or disable the view. Not available in ambient mode. |
| **Book Wall** | Every book finished in the current calendar year forms a 3D wall with about 10 books per column, in three to five columns. Books fall with physics onto a solid shelf without invisible lane walls. Page count affects the book's upright height, cover width, and spine thickness; title and author length also reserve spine space. Each spine is generated locally with a genre-based font and colors sampled from the selected cover; text always has at least 4.5:1 contrast against the spine. Each hardcover has a continuous casing that joins the front, spine, and back, with nearly square cover corners, straight spine ends, fine edge bevels, and an inset page block. The preferred cover keeps its aspect ratio on the upper board, with its top edge toward the left. Its satin surface follows a shallow physical bevel and meets a narrow edge blend outside the original artwork. Soft directional lighting reveals that edge while the book floats. After the library loads, the app prepares the current year's cover-derived spines, textures, and hardcover models before Book Wall opens. Opening Book Wall before preparation finishes shows a waiting dialog with Back to My Shelf; a failed spine offers Retry. The falling animation starts once the wall is ready. **Drop again** at the top right replays the fall with the prepared books. Each book stops moving after its supported landing, so later impacts do not bounce the settled stack. | Focus pulls a book straight out of its stack. Selecting it pulls the whole hardcover clear of neighboring books before moving and turning it toward the detail position. The full-screen canvas and synchronized camera movement keep its planned path within the screen without changing the book's dimensions. The 3D scene renders at up to 1080p even on 4K displays; spine lettering and cover art can appear softer, while native interface text stays at display resolution. The model remains there instead of a separate cover image, centered in a wider left column and vertically on the screen. It drifts up to 24 points vertically on a slow 40-second cycle, and turns 2° each way over a 60-second cycle. It rests turned to show its spine alongside the cover, tipped slightly forward to show its page edges, under a fixed key light, with its shadow on the wall behind it. At rest, a warm light pools on the wall behind the middle stacks in a dim room. The focused book's title and author appear on the shelf's front face; without focus, the shelf shows a hint. Focus pulls the book out and hangs a red bookmark ribbon from it, which slides out from under the book with a brief wave. A shallow groove marks the front board's hinge beside the spine. The camera fits the full sway. Closing details returns the model smoothly from its current pose and velocity. Detail text opens only after a successful flight; a failed opening leaves the wall usable. The detail page shows **Back to view** in the top-left corner over the wood background. Selecting it or pressing Back closes the detail page and returns the model to the shelf. Reduce Motion skips the fall and movement effects. Available on Apple TV 4K (2nd generation) and later; unavailable in ambient mode. |
| **Feed** | Hardcover activity from followed readers. Shows the latest update per book, including available reader, status, rating, date, and review data. Displayed edge-to-edge in paged groups of 4 with directional paging chevrons and complete, non-truncated book titles. | Enable or disable the view. Open available reviews. Ordering is fixed: newest first. |
| **Compare Shelves** | Your Hardcover library and one followed reader's library, in independently paged rows. The caption shows the selected book's title, author, and reading line beside both readers' profile photos, names, and star ratings. | In the top-right header, choose a reader (defaults to a random followed reader if unconfigured) and **Top rated · All time** (default) or **Recently read**. In **Settings → Views**, set the comparison limit with the minus and plus buttons in steps of five, from 5 to 40 (default 40). Enable or disable the view. |
| **Book Club** | Hardcover books that both you and the selected reader rated, with separate ratings and reviews. | Uses the same reader and count as **Compare Shelves** (synced across both screens). In the top-right header, choose a reader and sort by **Rating agreement** (default: highest average rating first) or **Rating disagreement** (largest rating difference first) via on-screen menu buttons; both options prioritize books with written review text from both readers before filling with other shared books. Review cards fill the bottom with uncluttered breathing room. Enable or disable the view; open long reviews or reveal spoilers. Review cards and the full review show each review's Hardcover like count; the full review also shows the reader's photo, name, and star rating. Spoiler visibility is an app-wide choice in **Settings → General**. |
| **Friends' Picks** | Hardcover books that people you follow rated 4 stars or higher and you haven't finished or marked read, from the libraries of up to 10 followed readers (the most active in your feed) and from feed ratings. Books liked by more readers come first, then higher average ratings, then title. Up to 40 books on one shelf; the caption shows the title, "Loved by" with up to three readers' photos and names, and the author with the readers' average rating. | Open details from a cover. Enable or disable the view. Available in ambient mode as a cover shelf. |

My Shelf and Year in Review captions show the focused book's format icons (both for **Physical & audiobook**) and title, its author, primary genre (the most-tagged English genre), and finish date, then its star rating. Compare Shelves captions show the author and reading line. The reading line is the format icon and name (**Physical**, **Audiobook**, **Ebook**, or **Physical & audiobook**), then the finish date.

Year in Review uses each book's most recent finish date, so a reread counts once, in the year of its latest finish. Page totals and averages skip books without page counts or ratings, and the tiles say how many books they cover. It ignores the **Settings → Shelf** collection and count.

Current reads lead **Default** and **All Books** in any sort or order and count toward **Books shown**. On My Shelf and its ambient shelf, a current read with progress shows it in the **Progress bars** style. **Vertical** dims the unread part of the cover, with light bleeding left from the reader's place. **Horizontal** shows the system progress bar along the bottom of the cover. Its caption adds "(Currently Reading)" after the title. My Shelf sorts dates and ratings highest first, titles and authors A–Z, and length shortest first. Missing values stay last, including when you reverse the order. CWA-only books appear under **Owned Books** or **All Books** because CWA does not provide reading history.

Social views require a connected, enabled Hardcover source and social permissions. Compare Shelves and Book Club default to a random followed reader when unconfigured, or you can choose a reader with the on-screen picker. In **Settings → Views**, **Show spoilers for read books** controls whether review spoilers are always hidden (default) or automatically revealed for books marked as read in your connected Hardcover account. See [Hardcover setup](docs/HARDCOVER_AUTHENTICATION.md).

## Other displays

| Display | Data source | User options |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Book details** | The selected book's prepared metadata and cached artwork. Selecting the rating histogram fetches up to 50 Hardcover community reviews, most liked first. | Open from a cover. Under the author, one line labels the reading format, publication year, and length, each with an icon, such as **Length: 400 pages**. The length is in pages, or in minutes for an audiobook when Hardcover records its listening time. Below it, one row holds the finish date when one is recorded, up to three English genres, and the rating histogram. Beside the histogram, your rating sits above the community average, such as "You ★3.5" above "Community ★4.2". The stars of the two ratings align, and the histogram draws only the bar for your rating in solid white. The author and each genre are buttons: selecting one closes details and filters My Shelf to that author's or genre's books, using the current shelf choices. A clear button with the author or genre name appears below the shelf caption; the filter is not saved. Back on the filtered shelf clears the filter and reopens the details. Book Wall details look and work the same, except that the book's 3D model replaces the cover, and a series book chosen there opens in cover details. For a Hardcover book in a series, a button beside the author, such as **#3 · Ana and Din Mysteries**, opens a pop-up with every main book in the series in order, two rows of seven per page with chevrons for further pages and full titles, each with its position and publication year; books you haven't read and books not yet released are faded, and selecting a book in your library opens its details. Select **Show more** to read a description that doesn't fit, full screen; Back returns to details. For Hardcover books with a connected account, select the histogram to browse reviews; each card shows five lines and the review's Hardcover like count, and selecting it opens the full review. Spoiler reviews follow the spoiler setting. No independent source, filter, or ordering settings. |
| **Ambient mode** | Prepared content from the views chosen on the Ambient page that have content. | Select **Ambient** in the sidebar. On that page, choose which views to include, listed in sidebar order (all by default, including **My Shelf**; Year in Review and Book Wall are not offered; hiding a view from the sidebar does not remove it; **Start ambient mode** is unavailable with none chosen), a view interval of 5, 10 (default), or 15 minutes and a session of 30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours, or **Until stopped** (default). Content changes every minute. When you finished books on today's date in earlier years, each visit to **My Shelf** first shows up to five of them, most recent year first, one per minute, with "N years ago today", the cover, and your rating. Select **Start ambient mode** to begin. |
| **Home Screen Top Shelf** | The first eligible books from My Shelf, in shelf order, so **Default** and **All Books** put current reads first. Current reads appear in a **Now reading** section and follow **Progress bars**: **Vertical** posters use the same light sweep as My Shelf, **Horizontal** uses the Top Shelf progress bar, and **Don't show** shows plain covers; other books appear under **Recently read**. CWA-only authenticated covers are excluded. | Uses My Shelf choices; no separate app settings. |

## Shared appearance

**Settings → General** provides **Serif** (default; New York, with regular text set at medium weight because it reads lighter than SF) or **Sans-serif** text throughout the app except the Ambient page and Settings, which always use the system font and shows a serif sample sentence beside the choice, **System** (default), **Light**, or **Dark** appearance, **Wood background** (enabled by default; toggle off for a plain dark or light background across all views), and four date formats: **Jan 01, 2026** (default), **01/31/2026**, **31/01/2026**, and **2026-01-31**. Dates use the selected format throughout the app. Ambient mode always uses a dark, dimmed presentation.

The retained generated-spine views and AI provider configuration remain disabled. Book Wall generates its spines with its [local spine method](docs/BOOK_WALL.md). See [navigation and rendering](docs/SOCIAL_VIEWS_AND_AMBIENT.md) for review and ambient behavior, and [focus and remote navigation](docs/FOCUS_NAVIGATION.md) for how the remote moves between items.
